See how it works

Buoy makes sure your CRM data is right, shows whether you can trust it on one simple customer journey view, and helps your leadership team understand what it means.

Ask three teams, get three numbers.

RevOps brings the numbers, and the meeting turns into a debate about whose number is right. The definitions were never written down, so the CRM drifts.

Can I trust the forecast?

Only if a stage means the same thing to every rep, and a close date reflects a real commitment.

Where do we lose deals?

Only if stage history and loss reasons are recorded the same way every time.

Why do customers churn?

Only if the CRM agrees with the system finance reports from.

Buoy takes that question out of the conversation.

Get it right

Get it right is the Audit, a one-time setup and cleanup in the Audit Workspace. Your process and definitions get written down there, and the CRM gets cleaned to match. At the end, you export everything.

Keep it right

Keep it right is Validation. Rules, plus a HubSpot extension, stop drift as reps work. AI fields fill your CRM from call transcripts and emails.

Know what it means is Intelligence. You get CRO-level insights and actions, delivered before you ask. They read your numbers against how your process is supposed to work.
