Why Buoy

A buoy marks the safe channel and keeps you off the rocks. Buoy does that for your revenue data: it marks how your process is supposed to run and flags when the CRM drifts.

From there, the work runs in three steps.

Step 1: The Audit

First we map your revenue funnel, then we audit your CRM and clean it up, so the revenue engine is built on solid ground.

Step 2: Validation + AI-filled properties

This is the enforcement layer that prevents drift. The HubSpot extension checks fields as reps work, and AI fills properties from transcripts and emails.
