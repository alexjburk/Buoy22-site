Why "Buoy"?

A buoy doesn't stop the ship. It marks a hazard in real time, while there's still room to turn — which is exactly the job this does inside a CRM: not blocking someone from saving a bad record, just flagging it clearly while there's still time to fix it. It's directional, too — the same marker works whether you're looking at a deal, a contact, or a support ticket.
